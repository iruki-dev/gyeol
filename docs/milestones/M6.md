# M6 — Coaching policy

`gyeol.coach` is pure logic over explanations and measured numbers: no ML and no audio. All user-facing text comes
from resource files: `resources/ko/coach.json` and the coach-authored `resources/ko/practice.json`. Policy norms
are in `resources/coach/norms.json`. A test checks that the coach code contains no Korean text.

## Delivered

- **Display thresholds, fitted and never hard-coded** (`coach.thresholds`):
  - An item is shown only if |magnitude| ≥ U(confidence) = max(MDC95, E95(confidence)).
    - MDC95 comes from retest pairs (Bland–Altman).
    - E95 is the 95 % error bound against the truth, binned by confidence and made monotone with the
      verification toolkit's `operating_threshold` machinery.
  - Below the lowest confidence seen in validation, nothing is shown.
  - An attribute without a fitted threshold is never shown; there is no numeric fallback.
  - Threshold files carry provenance, and every `Feedback` repeats it, so a synthetic fit announces itself.
- **Knob recovery** (`gyeol.eval.knob_recovery`, evaluation §5):
  - Synthetic takes with known per-note detune, onset shift and vibrato.
  - Each take is measured clean and in pink noise, which gives both retest pairs and labelled errors.
  - `examples/fit_thresholds.py --synthetic` writes a `ThresholdSet` marked `synthetic: true`.
- **Priority** (`coach.priority`):
  - The score is confidence × audibility.
  - Without audibility (no synthesis consent, or an item the renderer cannot correct), the whole ranking switches
    to confidence × |magnitude| / U(confidence). The two score kinds are never mixed.
  - Ties within 10 % use the brief's order: pitch offsets and intervals → rhythm → ornaments → dynamics →
    register/phonation (tentative) → diction (phrase level). Dynamics is not in the brief's list; I placed it
    before phonation because it needs no tentative wording.
- **Feedback volume and fading** (`coach.session`):
  - Each attempt gets one primary item and at most two expandable secondary items.
  - The frequency schedule (default full → half → quarter) advances when the attempt's error load has been stable
    for 3 attempts, and drops back to full as soon as the load rises.
  - Withheld attempts get an encouraging "listen for yourself" line.
- **Self-assessment first**: `Attempt.self_assessment_prompt()` asks which categories the user noticed. With
  `require_self_assessment`, `reveal()` refuses until an answer is recorded. The feedback then says whether the
  observation matched the primary item.
- **Session summary**: items that recur, items that improved, and items that look like habits (same direction in
  every attempt, or labelled `style_or_habit`), plus health notices.
- **Practice mapping** (`coach.practice`):
  - Lookup order: `attribute:status`, then `attribute:sign`, then `attribute`, then patterns like `quality_*`;
    `route:<id>` covers onboarding routes.
  - Exercises can be filtered by level and by `avoid_when` tags; fatigue avoids tagged exercises.
  - The initial exercise texts are an engineering draft (`reviewed_by: null`) and need review by a voice teacher.
- **Onboarding** (`coach.onboarding`, SSAP/SPB-style tasks: pitch matching, intervals, melody, discrimination):
  - **Production accuracy**: MAE, with octave-folded pitch matching, key-invariant melody intervals, and interval
    errors.
  - **Precision**: pooled within-target SD over repeated trials.
  - **Perception**: the smallest Δ at 75 % correct on an isotonic fit; "not reached" is reported, not guessed.
  - Band names are `on_target`/`developing`, `consistent`/`variable` and `fine`/`developing`. A test scans every
    Korean resource file for labelling words (음치, 박치, tone-deaf, amusia).
  - Routing: poor production with good perception goes to own-voice imitation plus wide-range pitch matching;
    developing perception adds listening practice; everyone else goes to the standard path.
- **Vocal-health guard** (`coach.health`):
  - `VoiceRange` (range and tessitura) and `check_phrase`: chooses the octave shift, flags phrases above or below
    the tessitura or out of range, and suggests a transposition in semitones.
  - Beginner restrictions: no coaching toward rough, fry or pressed/belt qualities, and no chest-register target
    above the tessitura (high belt).
  - `PhonationLog`: accumulated *voiced* seconds per session and per day.
  - `FatigueMonitor`: rising f0 instability, a falling top of range and increasing breath noise. Each is judged by
    comparing the first and last 3 attempts against the session's own detrended attempt-to-attempt noise
    (second-difference MAD), z > 3.
  - Every feedback and summary carries a persistent medical-referral notice.
- **Example**:
  - `examples/fit_thresholds.py --synthetic`
  - `examples/coach_demo_v2.py --coach thresholds.json [--level beginner] [--noticed pitch …]`: each take is an
    attempt, explained against all takes so far, then summarised.

## Verified (unit tests, `tests/test_m6_coach.py`)

- **Thresholds**:
  - The fitted MDC matches the simulated retest noise (±15 %).
  - U(c) is non-increasing in c, infinite below the evidence and floored at the MDC.
  - Pattern lookup works, the JSON round trip is lossless, and an empty `ThresholdSet` shows nothing.
- **Priority**: confidence × audibility ordering, the size-over-uncertainty fallback, and the full tie order;
  outside the tolerance the score wins.
- **Session**:
  - Volume is 1 + ≤ 2.
  - Self-assessment gating works and gives matched, missed or nothing.
  - Over 30 stable attempts the feedback rate settles at a quarter, and it returns to full on a worse attempt.
  - Tentative wording and consistency lines appear; the summary lists recurring, improved and habit items.
- **Practice**: status, sign and pattern keys; level and `avoid_when` filters; unknown exercise ids are rejected;
  every route has exercises.
- **Onboarding**:
  - An accurate singer (singing an octave low) is on target, consistent and fine, and routed standard.
  - Poor production with good perception gets own-voice imitation and wide-range matching.
  - Unreached perception is reported as "developing" and "not reached", and too few trials give no score.
- **Health**:
  - Octave choice, transposition suggestions and out-of-range detection work.
  - Beginner restrictions (rough, high belt) apply.
  - Phonation warnings fire at the norm.
  - Fatigue flags fire on trends but not on noise; the session shows them and keeps the referral notice.
  - `attempt_metrics` reads more breath noise from a breathy synthetic voice.
- **Integration**: with real synthetic singing, the coach picks the flat note and the late entry as the primary
  and secondary items, with practice attached.

## Limitations

- **Synthetic thresholds only.** The knob-recovery data are clean synthetic melodies, so the uncertainties are
  tiny (≈ 1.3 cents for intonation, ≈ 10 ms for onsets). Real thresholds need paired real recordings across
  devices and rooms (M8). `global_offset` has only one item per take and is "not reliable" with few takes.
- **No perceptual floor.** A measurably real 3-cent offset passes the threshold. Audibility ranks it low, but a
  JND-based floor needs listening data (M8).
- **Provisional norms.** The routing cut-offs in `norms.json` (poor production > 100 cents after Pfordresher &
  Brown 2007; perception and precision at 50 cents) and the phonation-time warnings are placeholders until there
  are app-population norms and a review by an SLP or voice teacher.
- **Onboarding scoring only.** Stimulus playback and trial capture are the app's job; this module scores the
  measured numbers.
- **Draft practice texts.** They need review by a voice teacher before release.
