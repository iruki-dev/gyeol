# M8 — Evaluation and hardening

## Delivered

- **RMVPE reimplementation** (`gyeol.pitch.rmvpe`):
  - Log-mel front end (128 HTK-mel bands, 30–8000 Hz, 10 ms hop).
  - Deep U-Net (5 encoder / 4 intermediate / 5 decoder stages), then a BiGRU and 360 sigmoid bins of 20 cents.
  - Local weighted-average decoding with a threshold of 0.03.
  - Module names follow the reference layout (741 state-dict keys, 90.4 M parameters), so weights fetched with
    `gyeol fetch rmvpe` load with a strict, key-by-key check.
  - `RMVPETracker` plugs into the pitch consensus and chunks long audio on frame boundaries.
  - No weights are bundled or downloaded. An optional test compares against real weights when
    `GYEOL_RMVPE_WEIGHTS` is set.
- **Regression heads** (closes the M7 TODO):
  - `TaskSpec(kind="regression")` outputs a heteroscedastic Gaussian (mean and log-variance).
  - The variance scale is fitted by NLL on held-out singers.
  - Confidence is σ_ref / (σ_ref + σ).
  - Promoted continuous attributes (`PromotedAttribute.task_spec()`) now train as heads.
- **Robustness grid** (`gyeol.eval.robustness`):
  - Conditions: noise, reverb, band-limit, codecs (when ffmpeg exists), separation artefacts, Bluetooth, and
    backing-track bleed. There is a `short` grid for CI and a `full` research grid.
  - Each condition is analysed and explained exactly as the app would, including latency refinement for Bluetooth.
  - Per curve and per explanation item: ICC(2,1) with a CI, MDC95, Bland–Altman bias, the median |Δ| from clean per
    condition, how often each item is found at all under each condition, and operating thresholds along multi-level
    axes.
- **Expert benchmarks and coach agreement** (`gyeol.eval.benchmarks`):
  - VocalCoachBench ([arXiv:2609.04241](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04241)) is registered `noncommercial`. Its
    sources include the Smule Research Data License and CC BY-NC-SA, and the annotation license is not stated, so it
    loads under the **research profile only**.
  - The loader uses a field map (the release schema is not in the paper), and malformed clips are reported.
  - Scoring:
    - Top-k issue-label identification against the label-prior baseline.
    - Segment recall per label and precision.
    - Only clips with a reference performance are scored, because gyeol explains against a target.
  - `fleiss_kappa`, `cohen_kappa` and `coach_agreement` are the analysis for `docs/protocols/coach_agreement.md`.
    The retention-study design is in `docs/protocols/retention_study.md`.
- **Listening calibration** (`gyeol.eval.listening`):
  - Isotonic detection rate vs M5 audibility gives the score at 75 % detection (2AFC).
  - `ThresholdSet.audibility_floor` makes the coach drop items that are measurable but inaudible (closes the M6
    "no perceptual floor" gap once listening data exist).
  - Protocol: `docs/protocols/listening_test.md` §3.
- **Model cards** (`gyeol.eval.model_card`):
  - Built from checkpoint metadata, so the license lineage cannot be typed in by hand: effective tag, each source's
    registry entry, profile and config hash.
  - Evaluation tables carry a `synthetic` flag.
  - `validate()` refuses a card with non-commercial sources under the commercial profile, missing required
    out-of-scope uses (no target-singer synthesis, no diagnosis, no singer identification), or synthetic-only or
    unverified claims the limitations do not admit.
- **ONNX export and latency** (`gyeol.export`, extra `[onnx]`: onnx Apache-2.0, onnxscript and onnxruntime MIT):
  - Five graphs: attribute heads, RMVPE network, acoustic model, vocoder harmonic path (noise streams as inputs) and
    singer encoder. They use opset 18 and the dynamo exporter, with dynamic batch and time.
  - Each is checked with ONNX Runtime at a second batch size and length. The TorchScript exporter was rejected: it
    bakes sequence lengths into attention reshapes, which exactly this check caught.
  - Every graph gets a JSON sidecar with its verification and provenance.
  - Latency: `profile` / `profile_onnx`, plus per-stage timings of `analyze` (`rep.meta["timings_s"]`) and
    `gyeol profile`.
- **Example**: `examples/evaluate_and_export.py` writes a robustness report, ONNX graphs, `latency.md` and a model
  card.

## Fixed along the way

- **Bluetooth augmentation shifted pitch** (M2 bug, found by the grid). A smoothly wandering delay resampled the
  audio, a Doppler effect of up to ~65–100 cents, so every Bluetooth condition looked "out of tune". Real routes
  absorb jitter in a buffer; delay jumps at re-syncs, and clock drift is tens of ppm. The augmentation now uses
  piecewise-constant delays on packet boundaries plus ±50 ppm drift. There is a regression test.
- **Vocoder phase drift.** The oscillator phase was accumulated in float32. Long renders drifted, and PyTorch and
  ONNX disagreed by 8e-4 after 0.3 s. It is now accumulated in float64 and wrapped to two cycles, which keeps the
  ½-rate subharmonic continuous. Parity is 4e-7 on 10 s of audio.
- **Invariance report and missing conditions.** A condition where a dimension was never measured (an item withheld
  as "cannot judge" at 10 dB SNR) used to discard the dimension entirely. It is now left out of that dimension's
  ICC (`conditions_used`), and its absence shows in `presence`.

## Measured (synthetic singing; untrained networks for latency)

**Explanation robustness**: 4 performances × 7 conditions (short grid, no codecs).

| Item | ICC(2,1) | MDC95 | Worst condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intonation offset (−40…+35 cents) | 1.00 | 2.1 cents | separation (|Δ| 1.9) |
| Onset timing (−60…+90 ms) | 0.99 | 15 ms | reverb T60 0.5 s (|Δ| 13) |
| Breathiness (signal layer) | — (no variation between performances) | 4–9 dB | pink noise at 30 dB SNR (|Δ| 3–8 dB) |

At 10 dB SNR the intonation item for the detuned note is withheld ("cannot judge") in every performance rather than
guessed.

**Latency**: 4 CPUs, production-size untrained networks, 10 s of audio, median of 3 runs.

| Component | PyTorch | ONNX Runtime |
|---|---|---|
| Vocoder, harmonic path | 1202 ms (RTF 0.12) | 722 ms (RTF 0.072) |
| RMVPE network (90 M parameters) | 451 ms (RTF 0.045) | 253 ms (RTF 0.025) |
| Signal-layer analysis (DSP trackers) | RTF 0.07–0.08, of which pitch tracking is ~85 % | — |

## Findings

- **Signal-layer breathiness is not robust to noise.** Pink noise at 30 dB SNR moves the aperiodic ratio by several
  dB, more than typical user-to-target differences. Until trained phonation heads prove more robust in this grid,
  fitted display thresholds (M6) will, and should, suppress most breathiness items on noisy recordings.
- **Reverb, not noise, limits onset timing.** A 0.5 s T60 moves some onsets by 20–35 ms.
- **ICC needs variation between performances.** Items with the same value in every performance get ICC ≈ 0 even
  when they are perfectly stable. Read ICC for dimensions that vary, and MDC95 / |Δ| for the rest.
- **Pitch tracking dominates CPU time.** RMVPE via ONNX (RTF 0.025) would be faster than the three DSP trackers
  together.

## Not done / limitations

- **No real data, no trained weights.** Robustness, benchmark and agreement numbers on real Korean singing, and
  RMVPE checked against its released weights, all wait on data and fetched weights.
- **VocalCoachBench.** The adapter's field map is a placeholder until the release is downloaded and inspected. Its
  recordings are English and mostly lack a reference performance, so only part of the benchmark can score gyeol.
- **Export scope.** The vocoder's aperiodic noise branch (inverse STFT) and the front ends stay outside ONNX, as
  documented.
- **Latency profile.** From one 4-CPU machine; numbers vary by hardware. The report records the environment so
  profiles can be compared.
- **Norms and thresholds.** The retention study and coach agreement are designed, not run. The coach's norms and
  thresholds (M6) remain provisional until they are.
