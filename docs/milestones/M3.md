# M3 — Attribute heads

## Delivered

- **Frame encoders** (`gyeol.encoders`):
  - `TorchSSLEncoder`: wraps any frozen model that uses torchaudio's `extract_features` convention, averages the
    selected layers (low/mid for phonation, upper for content) and projects them onto the shared grid.
  - `ssl_from_checkpoint("hubert_base" | "wavlm_base", path, asset, layers)`: builds the torchaudio architecture and
    loads a **local** state dict fetched by the user. The license gate runs first; nothing is downloaded.
  - `DSPFrameFeatures`: a no-weights baseline built from the signal-layer curves (pitch centre, voicing,
    aperiodicity, subharmonics, loudness, vibrato and content), with a validity flag per curve.
- **Heads** (`gyeol.attributes.heads`):
  - `FrameHeads`: a temporal-conv trunk plus task heads:
    - `register`: softmax over chest / mixed / falsetto;
    - `phonation`: sigmoids over breathy / pressed_belt / pharyngeal_twang / fry / rough;
    - `laryngeal`: lenis / aspirated / fortis;
    - `phones`: an optional Korean phone set.
  - `CalibratedHeads` turns predictions into attribute curves:
    - temperatures are applied per task;
    - frames that either OOD detector flags are **unknown** (NaN, confidence 0);
    - confidence is multiplied by the frontend's per-frame quality factor, and by voicing for register/phonation.
- **Calibration and OOD**:
  - Temperature scaling is fitted on **held-out singers** only (`fit_temperature`), and `expected_calibration_error`
    measures the result.
  - `MahalanobisOOD` runs on both the penultimate embedding and the input features, because LayerNorm hides input
    scale from the embedding. Its threshold is a quantile of calibration-singer scores.
- **Training** (`gyeol.train.heads.train_heads`):
  - Deterministic, plain-PyTorch training on singer-disjoint train / calibration splits.
  - Masked multi-task losses: weak utterance labels are broadcast over voiced frames, and unlabelled qualities are
    masked per element.
- **Integration**: `AnalysisConfig(heads=..., feature_encoder=...)` adds the learned curves to the `Representation`.
  A failure is reported as a warning, never as silent defaults.
- **Probing** (`gyeol.eval.probes`):
  - Singer-grouped K-fold logistic probes (accuracy vs majority chance, with a binomial p-value) and ridge probes
    (R²).
  - `leakage()` gives a "near chance" verdict, and `probe_battery()` runs every layer × target combination.
- Checkpoints carry license provenance (tested with VocalSet plus AI Hub, which yields `commercial_ok_conditional`).
- Pitch consensus now also treats **strong harmonic salience** as voicing evidence. Breathy voices make the
  YIN-family trackers abstain even when the harmonics are clearly present.

## Measured on the synthetic corpus (8 singers × 3 registers × 3 phonations; tests)

Tiny heads (hidden 32) on DSP baseline features, evaluated on held-out singers:

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Register accuracy | ≈ 0.79 (chance 0.33) |
| Breathy / pressed accuracy | ≈ 0.79 / 0.66 |
| ECE, raw → calibrated | 0.13 → 0.07 |
| Garbage features marked unknown | 100 % (0 % of in-distribution frames) |

These numbers show that the machinery works. **They say nothing about real singing.** Real heads must be trained on
AI Hub / VocalSet / own data with SSL features, and compared against this DSP baseline.

## Not done

- No SSL weights are bundled or fetched. Registry entries still need verified URLs and checksums before
  `gyeol fetch` can download them.
- MERT and Whisper encoders are not wrapped yet (`TorchSSLEncoder` accepts any compatible module).
- The RMVPE reimplementation moved to M8, because it needs weights to verify against.
- The diction head needs forced-aligned phone labels (MFA, Korean). The `phones` task and training loop are ready.
