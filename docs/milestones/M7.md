# M7 — Discovery

`gyeol.discover` looks for what the interpretable layer does not yet cover. It never talks to the coach directly: a
discovered feature reaches users only after it passes the promotion rule and is trained as an attribute head.

## Delivered

- **TopK sparse autoencoder** (`discover.sae`), after Gao et al. 2024:
  - TopK activation (k active latents per frame), a decoder with unit-norm rows, tied initialisation, and input
    standardisation stored with the fit;
  - an AuxK loss that revives dead latents;
  - deterministic for a given seed;
  - `feature_stats` reports how often each latent fires, its mean activation and its share of the reconstruction
    energy.
- **Matching** (`discover.match`):
  - A separation score per latent × target: `|2·AUROC − 1|` for classes (one-vs-rest, with class and sign),
    `|Spearman ρ|` for continuous targets such as v0.1 DSP features.
  - Unknown frames (NaN, or `None` for categories) are skipped.
  - `MatchTable.novel()` lists latents that match nothing known, ranked by energy: the candidates to look at.
- **Conditional directions** (`discover.directions`):
  - On/off pairs are aligned with the content warp (`pair_frames`, `pair_from_example` for M2 `PairedExample`s).
  - f0 (linear and quadratic) and loudness are **regressed out** on all off and on frames together.
  - For each f0 band × loudness band × vowel cell: the mean difference, its size, sign consistency and a
    per-singer t statistic. Thin cells fall back to a pooled direction.
  - `score()` projects new frames onto their cell's direction; `similarity()` shows how much the direction depends
    on the condition.
- **Transfer tests and the promotion rule** (`discover.transfer`):
  - A linear readout (logistic or ridge) is tested on held-out **singers**, held-out **pitch bands** (every band,
    including both extremes, must pass; the worst band decides) and held-out **languages** (required when language
    labels exist).
  - Each is compared with an in-distribution K-fold over recordings.
  - Scores are computed over units (recordings, or notes), not frames: gain is `2·AUROC − 1` with a one-sided
    Mann–Whitney test, or Spearman ρ for continuous targets.
  - A split passes when p < α, gain ≥ `min_gain` and gain ≥ (1 − `max_drop`) × the in-distribution gain.
  - `PromotionRegistry` (JSON) accepts only passed decisions with both singer and pitch-range results. It stores the
    readout and all evidence, and refuses duplicates.
  - `PromotedAttribute.task_spec()` gives the M3 head spec for binary attributes.
- **Residual-energy monitoring** (`discover.residual`):
  - `residual_energy` / `representation_residual_energy` measure the mean ‖r‖² over voiced frames.
  - `ResidualMonitor` compares slices (dataset, technique, genre, app version) with a baseline using a robust
    log-scale z-score, fits a Theil–Sen trend with a 95 % CI, and raises the flags `coverage_gap:<slice>` and
    `residual_rising`.
- **Example**: `examples/discover_demo.py` runs the whole workflow on synthetic singing.

## Verified (`tests/test_m7_discover.py`)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Planted dictionary (24 atoms, 3 active) | ≥ 90 % of atoms recovered (cos > 0.9), FVU < 0.1, TopK sparsity, deterministic |
| Matching | a planted label latent (score > 0.9), a planted continuous latent (with sign), dead latent scores 0; NaN and `None` skipped; novel list correct |
| Directions | with an f0 confound (on takes sung 40 cents higher) the regressed direction recovers the true one (cos > 0.95) and beats the naive one; different directions per f0 band are recovered; per-singer t > 5 |

| Pitch confound | a pitch-confounded feature passes held-out singers but fails held-out pitch bands and is refused |

| Language confound | a language-confounded feature fails when language labels are given |
| Continuous target | promoted; its M3 head is a TODO(M8) |

## Findings worth keeping

- **Per-recording normalisation erases recording-level phonation.** With the DSP features (MFCC content is CMVN'd
  per recording, and loudness is relative to the median), no SAE latent could separate breathy from normal takes,
  and the promotion rule correctly refused. Discovery inputs must keep absolute spectral-shape information. Frozen
  SSL phonation layers (M3 adapters) or frame-normalised log-mel spectra do.
- **Unit counts limit what can be proven.** With 12 recordings (recordings as units), the per-band Mann–Whitney
  test for a real effect only reached p = 0.066. Notes as units, or more recordings, are needed. The rule fails closed: "cannot
  test" means "not promoted".
- **A technique's direction depends on pitch.** The breathiness direction differs between f0 bands (cosine 0.42),
  which supports fitting directions per condition cell rather than one global direction.

## Not done / limitations

- **No real data.** Discovery on AI Hub, VocalSet and own recordings, and on real residuals from a trained M4
  autoencoder, needs that data and those weights. The residual monitor was verified on synthetic energies only.
- **Pooled out-of-fold scores.** Transfer tests pool readout scores across folds. This is standard, but with very
  few units per fold the readout's scale can vary between folds; the unit-level tests are therefore conservative.
- **Promotion criteria are configurable statistics, not tuned policy.** Defaults are α = 0.05,
  `min_gain` = 0.2 and `max_drop` = 0.5, with no multiple-comparison correction across candidates. When many
  latents are screened, apply Holm (`verification.stats.holm`) to their p-values.
- **Continuous heads.** Promoted continuous attributes cannot yet be trained as M3 heads (TODO(M8)).
- **Vowel cells.** They need phone posteriors or aligned lyrics; the demo uses f0 cells only.
